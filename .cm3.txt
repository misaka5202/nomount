ci: exclude generated/ when overlaying kheaders, clean tree beforehand

The overlay pulled kheaders' include/generated/* (autoconf.h,
utsrelease.h, compile.h, ...) into the source tree. Those are outputs of
a previous build, and their presence makes kbuild abort with

  *** The source tree is not clean, please run make ARCH=arm64 mrproper

which is why this step failed as soon as the header overlay was enabled.

Skip generated/ and config/ during the copy (they must be regenerated
from the current .config), and drop stale build products before the
overlay runs. Also fix the destination paths: the step now cds into
common/ so include/ and arch/arm64/include/ are addressed relative to it
rather than through a common/ prefix.
