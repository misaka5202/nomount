fix: keep BTF, drop the invented MITIGATION_ITS fields

Two controlled data points now exist for module_layout:

    A) BTF off, ITS on  -> 0xed15e86a
    B) BTF on,  ITS on  -> 0x831c167a
    device              -> 0xea759d7f

Turning BTF on moved the CRC, confirming CONFIG_DEBUG_INFO_BTF_MODULES
really does shape struct module. Neither combination matches, and the
remaining untested variable is the ITS block itself.

/proc/config.gz and kheaders' own include/generated/autoconf.h both lack
CONFIG_MITIGATION_ITS, so the shipped kernel does not enable it. The
module.h inside kheaders does contain the block, which is why it was
mistaken for a real difference -- but kheaders has already been shown
not to reflect the shipped build (sizeof(struct module): 1088 on device
vs 1024 from that header set). /proc/config.gz is the reliable source.

So stop editing struct module entirely: remove the ITS injection and its
Kconfig entry, keep the device's BTF settings, and patch only the
resolve_btfids toolchain issue. That leaves config exactly as the device
reports it, which is the premise GKI KMI stability relies on.
